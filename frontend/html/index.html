Civic repair prototype

Report a local problem. Decide what gets fixed. Check the work.

Proof of Fix is a small working system for neighbourhood repairs. Residents can submit an issue, vote on priorities, and review the finished work before the remaining payment is released.

How a repair moves through the system

Each role has a limited job. The ward office cannot mark its own work as accepted, and a contractor cannot release the final payment.

A resident reports a problem

They add a photo, location, description, and a rough cost. The ward office reviews the report before it enters a vote.

Residents choose a priority

Each resident receives a limited credit balance. Supporting one issue more strongly costs progressively more credits.

The selected repair is funded

Half of the approved amount is paid when work starts. The other half is held in the treasury contract.

Residents review the finished work

The contractor posts an update and opens a completion vote. Approval releases the balance. Rejection returns it to the treasury.

Why the payment is split

A contractor needs an advance to begin work. Paying the full amount before anyone checks the result removes the useful part of public review.

Proof of Fix sends the advance first and keeps the balance in a Sepolia smart contract. The contract settles only after the completion vote has closed.

What this project contains

The browser app uses Clerk for accounts and Neon Postgres for reports and profiles. Solidity contracts handle voting rounds and escrow settlement on Sepolia.

The database keeps descriptive records. Contract transactions remain separately visible on the public chain.
